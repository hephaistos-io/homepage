## Summary

Pressiert has no account, no advertising, no analytics of its own and no tracking. Your favourites and settings stay on your devices, and no data about you reaches the developer. To show departures, the app asks the timetable service search.ch, which also receives your location when you look for stops nearby.

## What data is collected

- Favourites and settings: your favourite stations, their filters, your widget settings and your commute hours. Stored only on your iPhone, and passed directly to your paired Apple Watch through Apple's watch connection.
- Usage record: when you open a station. Stored only on your iPhone. You can turn it off with "Suggest from my usage" in the app.
- Location: only while you use the app and only with your permission. Not stored. For nearby stops, your coordinates are sent to search.ch; for widgets showing your nearest favourite, your location is compared with your favourites on the device and not sent anywhere.
- Timetable requests: the station you open or the text you search for, sent to search.ch. With each request, search.ch also receives your IP address and the app's name and version, but no identifier of you or your device.
- Tips: in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment details or personal data, only Apple's anonymous sales reports.
- Crash reports and usage statistics: only if you chose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings. Apple provides them to the developer anonymously.

Data on your devices may be part of your device backup, depending on your backup settings, and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Why it is used

- Favourites and settings: to show your stations and widgets the way you set them up.
- Usage record: to suggest commute hours, during which widgets refresh more often.
- Location: to find stops near you, and to choose the favourite nearest to you in widgets.
- Timetable requests: to load departures and find stations.
- Tips: to process your purchase.
- Crash reports and usage statistics: to find and fix problems in the app.

## Third parties

- search.ch (Swisscom Directories AG): the stations you open, your search text, your coordinates when you look for stops nearby, your IP address and the app's name and version · [search.ch privacy policy](https://search.ch/privacy)
- Apple: tip purchases, and crash reports and usage statistics if you chose to share them · [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Where data is processed

Timetable requests and your coordinates for nearby stops go to search.ch (Swisscom Directories AG, a Swiss company). Purchases and shared analytics are processed by Apple under its own privacy policy. Everything else stays on your devices.

## Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can request information about your data and its correction or deletion. Since the developer holds no data about you, there is usually nothing to disclose; for any questions, see the [contact page](/contact).

## Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this notice will be updated and its date changed.
